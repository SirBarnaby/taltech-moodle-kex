# Three Moodle KEX epics: implementation handover

The three proposals in `2026-10-20-local-recon.md` are implemented in the local `codex/kex-interactions` checkout. This continues the existing work; no commits were created, no files were reset, and nothing was published. The desktop and signed-in Firefox profile were not controlled.

## Navigation and viewport ownership

`src/fx/transactions.ts` coordinates page, topic, filter, history and reader intents, cancellation, readiness, focus and effects scheduling. Destination HTML has a coordinator-owned lifetime: changing a topic or opening a reader preserves the current page's pending document, while changing routes aborts an obsolete document request. This prevents fast interactions from leaving the loading indicator stuck or dropping course summaries.

The persistent shell remains mounted. Transition cleanup releases inert visual copies and height locks, including failed readiness. The viewport helper bounds movement per native animation frame and cancels when the user takes over. History records the actual selected topic even on the initial URL without a hash; cold Back/Forward waits for usable destination content. Reader cleanup restores the originating viewport only while that reader still owns it, and closing or superseding the reader aborts its pending read.

## Effects runtime and stable first paint

`src/fx/effects.ts` owns deferred/compiling/painted/live/paused/static/fallback states, a shared 30 Hz frame pump, two live surfaces and a 4 ms CPU submission budget. Decorative work yields during navigation. Resizes are repainted after the interaction, hidden documents stop drawing, and stale callbacks cannot paint disposed surfaces. Switching effects off pauses an existing context; re-enabling preserves its phase. Unmount disposes its resources.

Hero and page-band fallbacks now match every faculty palette, shape, intensity and initial phase. Login retains its separate portrait asset. The shader measures glow widths in its reference artwork space, so changing resolution or entering a compact course header does not broaden its glow. Faculty palette changes replace the canvas with the renderer, preventing reuse of a disposed WebGL context. All thirteen fallbacks can be regenerated from the actual shader and faculty definitions with `scripts/render-fallback.mjs`. Activity thumbnails remain static and perform no shader compilation or pixel readback.

Shared motion tokens cover reveals, press/focus feedback, overlays, effects scheduling and the 700 ms accent delay.

## Moodle parity and repeatable release checks

The typed adapters identify authority, freshness and original-Moodle destinations for notices, summaries, deadlines, grades, navigation and the academic calendar. The stock block inventory maps known widgets and retains escapes for unknown widgets, including dashboards containing only notices or unknown blocks. The finite academic calendar uses teaching weeks 1–16 and the published session/break boundaries, with a visible maintenance requirement outside its reviewed coverage.

`npm run verify:release` validates source, tests, build provenance and manifest, builds both local extension entry points, then runs the real-theme replay in isolated headless Edge. Every run has its own port, server identity, build marker, screenshots and results. A final hash check rejects a changed source tree or mismatching root/dist bundle. The replay preserves captured progress and deadline data.

The test date fixture changes `Date` only. Native timers and animation frames remain intact; a simulated scheduler was found to produce misleading scroll jumps. The unchanged deep-scroll movement limit passed five consecutive runs after that correction.
