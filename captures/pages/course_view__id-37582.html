  • Welcome to The Basics of Embedded Systems (BES) course. This course targets modern 32-bit microcontroller (ARM Cortex-M) architecture and software (SW) design methods for performance and energy-efficient embedded systems.

  • Announcements and news 1 lugemata postitus

  • Pre-course test

    • Will be proceeded during the first week / first lab date of the course during lab time.
    • The purpose: Get early feedback about the course participants background and readiness (level of earlier studies) for the course, also expectations about study outcomes.
    • The test is written and will take about 1 hour.
